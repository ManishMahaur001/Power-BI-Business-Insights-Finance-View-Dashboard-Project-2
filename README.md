# 📊 Business Insights 360 – Finance View Project 2 

## 📌 Overview

**Business Insights 360 – Finance View** is a Power BI dashboard designed to analyze financial performance through **Sales, Gross Margin, Net Profit, P&L, Region, Market, Customer and Product analysis**.

The objective is to convert business data into **actionable insights for better decision-making**.

---

## 🎯 Key Business Questions

* Are Sales growing compared to LY and Target?
* Is the business generating profitable growth?
* Where are we losing margin?
* Which regions, markets and customers are performing well?
* Which products are driving revenue and profitability?
* What is causing the difference between Actual and Target?

---

## 📊 Dashboard Components

* **Net Sales, Gross Margin & Net Profit KPIs**
* **P&L Analysis**
* **LY vs Target Benchmark**
* **Performance Trend Analysis**
* **Region → Market → Customer Analysis**
* **Segment → Category → Product Analysis**
* **YTD / YTG & Time-based Analysis**

---

# 🔎 Business Analysis

The dashboard follows:

**What happened → Why did it happen → What should we do?**

### 📈 Sales Analysis

**Problem:** Sales are below LY/Target.

**Analysis:** Drill down into Region → Market → Customer → Product to find the major contributors.

**Action:** Focus on underperforming markets, customer recovery and pricing/product strategy.

### 💰 Profitability Analysis

**Problem:** Sales are increasing but Gross Margin % or Net Profit % is declining.

**Analysis:** Check COGS, deductions, product mix and operating expenses.

**Action:** Improve pricing, reduce unnecessary costs and focus on profitable products/customers.

### 🏭 Cost Analysis

**Problem:** COGS is growing faster than Sales.

**Analysis:** Analyze Manufacturing, Freight and Other Costs.

**Action:** Optimize suppliers, production and logistics costs.

### 👥 Customer Analysis

Identify **high-revenue/high-profit** and **high-revenue/low-profit** customers.

**Action:** Retain profitable customers and review pricing/contracts for low-margin customers.

### 📦 Product Analysis

Identify products with **high sales but low margins** or declining performance.

**Action:** Reprice, optimize product mix or reduce low-performing products.

### 🌍 Region & Market Analysis

Identify the regions and markets responsible for positive or negative performance.

**Action:** Replicate successful strategies and create targeted recovery plans for weak markets.

## 🚨 Problem → Solution

| Problem                         | Analysis                           | Solution                           |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Sales below Target              | Region/Customer/Product drill-down | Sales recovery strategy            |
| Sales ↑ but Profit ↓            | Margin & P&L analysis              | Pricing + cost optimization        |
| COGS ↑ faster than Sales        | Cost analysis                      | Supplier & logistics optimization  |
| High deductions                 | Deduction analysis                 | Improve commercial controls        |
| High sales, low-margin customer | Customer profitability             | Pricing/contract review            |
| High sales, low-margin product  | Product analysis                   | Repricing/product-mix optimization |
